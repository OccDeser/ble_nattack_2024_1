\section{Results} \label{sec:6results}
% 本节将介绍三个案例的分析结果，并分别与文献的模型进行对比。对于BC模型发现的新攻击，我们将详细描述攻击的过程。
This section presents the analysis results of the previous three cases and compares them with models from the literature.
We will provide a detailed description of the process for the novel attack discovered from the BC model analysis results.

\subsection{WPA2-PSK's 4-way handshake}
% 在\cite{cremers2020wpa2}中，作者开展了对WPA2的四步握手协议的形式化分析，展示了四步握手协议在key reinstallation attacks下的脆弱性。尽管他们的研究重点是nonce reuse对协议的影响，我们使用本文提出的方案，对他们的形式化模型稍加修改，赋予攻击者妥协低熵密钥的能力，以分析常用口令的滥用造成的安全问题。
% 我们的模型在发现密钥重装攻击的同时，也发现了PMKID攻击，这个攻击在原来的模型中是发现不了的。
In \cite{cremers2020wpa2}, a formal analysis of the WPA2 four-way handshake protocol is conducted, revealing its vulnerability to key reinstallation attacks.
While the original study primarily focus on the impact of nonce reuse on protocols, we made slight modifications to their formal model to apply our modelling methods proposed in this paper.
These modifications enable the attacker to compromise low-entropy keys, allowing us to investigate the security vulnerabilities arising from the misuse of common passwords.
This approach provides a comprehensive understanding of the potential risks associated with low-entropy key exploitation.
Our model is capable of identifying key reinstallation attacks and has also uncovered the PMKID attack, a vulnerability that was not apparent in the original model.


\subsection{BLE's pairing}
% 使用tamarin-prover针对BLE设备配对协议的形式化分析工作有很多\cite{shi2023blesc, jangid2023blepe}，其中\cite{shi2023blesc}的模型最为全面，建模了BLE设备配对的所有关联模型。该工作分析了BLE配对协议的四个认证属性，与两个保护属性，和长期密钥的保密性。尽管在他们的模型中，作者建模了暴力破解攻击，但是这是一个取巧的方法，因为他们过度简化了配对协议中传输密钥的过程。我们修改了他们的模型，添加了BLE设备建立加密会话，并使用加密信道传输密钥的过程。在这个添加了加密会话的模型上，我们应用了本文的低熵密钥破解建模方法。
There are many formal analysis works\cite{shi2023blesc, jangid2023blepe} on BLE-SC pairing protocols using the Tamarin-prover.
Among these studies, the model presented in \cite{shi2023blesc} stands out as the most comprehensive, encompassing all association models during pairing.
This work introduced several security properties, including four authentication properties, MITM protection, LTK confusion protection, and the secrecy of LTK.
However, the approach taken in this work to model brute-force attacks is tricky and imprecise due to the oversimplification of the key transmission process within the pairing protocol.
To address this limitation, we have revised their model to incorporate the establishment of encrypted sessions for BLE devices and the transmission of keys over the protected channel.
Upon this, we apply the low-entropy key cracking oracle proposed in this paper, thereby offering a more general analysis of the protocol's security.

\subsection{BC's session establishment}
% 我们针对BC的会话建立协议进行了大规模的验证，通过分析两种攻击模型下的所有场景的模型。
We performed a full-scale study of the session establishment protocol of BC by analyzing all scenarios under the 2 attack models.
There are 9 device types studied for BC, resulting in a total of 81 possible scenarios.
In these scenarios, the pairing process between an SCOnly device and a LegacyOnly device has no chance to be completed without an attacker.
Thus, analyzing the subsequent session establishment in this kind of scenarios is unnecessary.
This leaves 63 ($9 \times 9 \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{3} \times 2\right)$) scenarios to analyze.
% 我们的实验一个运行Ubuntu 22.04的服务器上完成，该服务器有一颗AMD 7950x3d CPU，配备 196GB 的 DDR5 内存。我们在\autoref{tab:bc-session-result}总结了我们的分析结果。我们找到了x中会话场景违反了session key 的隐私性，其中x中场景的违反隐私性的trace表明存在密钥降级攻击。我们从剩余x种场景的反例trace中发现了一个新攻击，我们称之为单侧密钥降级。
% 我们的实验在两台服务器上完成，一台运行Ubuntu 22.04，配备AMD 7950X3D CPU和192GB RAM，验证QA假设下的所有会话场景；另一台运行Ubuntu 22.04，配备Intel i9-14900K CPU和192GB RAM，验证SA假设下的所有会话场景。我们在\autoref{tab:bc-session-result}中总结了我们的分析结果。
Our experiment was conducted on a server running Ubuntu 22.04 with an AMD 7950X3D CPU and 192GB RAM.
The results of our analysis are summarized in \autoref{tab:bc-session-result}.
Our findings indicate that x session scenarios compromised the secrecy of the session key, with traces from x scenarios revealing the key negotiation downgrade attack\cite{antonioli2019knob}.
Additionally, from the counterexample traces of the remaining x scenarios, we identified a novel attack, which we term as the Unilateral Multi-Downgrade(UMD) attack.

\subsubsection{Unilateral Multi-Downgrade Attack} 
% 我们的模型发现了这个新攻击，其中，攻击者多次降级一侧设备的密钥长度，以分段地恢复出完整的会话密钥。在一个理想的BC会话建立协议中，一个仅接受16字节密钥的中心设备和接受1-16字节的边缘设备协商的会话密钥长度为16字节，攻击者很难通过暴力手段破解。但是，攻击者首先将双方降级到Legacy会话，然后将边缘设备的密钥降级到8字节，暴力破解出8字节的会话密钥。然后，重新与边缘设备建立会话，将会话密钥升级到16字节，这样，攻击者就可以通过暴力破解的方式破解出剩余的8字节会话密钥。整个过程如图\autoref{fig:bc-umd}所示。
We discover this novel attack where an attacker incrementally downgrades the key length of a device on one side to incrementally recover the complete session key.
In an ideal BC session establishment procedure, a central device that only accepts a 16-byte key and a peripheral device that accommodates key lengths from 1 to 16 bytes negotiate a session key length of 16 bytes, rendering brute-force attacks impractical.
Nevertheless, the attacker initiates the attack by downgrading both devices to a Legacy session, subsequently reducing the peripheral device's key length to 8 bytes.
This reduction allows the attacker to brute-force the 8-byte session key.
Following this, the session is re-established with the peripheral device, upgrading the session key to 16 bytes.
This upgrade enables the attacker to brute-force the remaining 8 bytes of the session key.
The complete process is depicted in \autoref{fig:bc-umd}.
\begin{figure}[hbtp]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.945285\columnwidth]{img/umd-attack} 
    \caption{Unilateral Multi-Downgrade Attack}
    \label{fig:bc-umd}
\end{figure}
% the UMD attack 有很多变体，攻击者可以根据自己的计算能力和受害设备最低支持的密钥长度，自由选择逐步降级的次数。假设攻击者选择的降级次数为$n$，每次降级的大小间隔一致，那么攻击者的计算次数为$2^{\frac{128}{n}}n$，当n为16时，攻击者暴力破解16字节的会话密钥的难度从$2^{128}$次降到$2^{12}$次。
The UMD attack encompasses several variations, allowing the attacker to freely choose the number of downgrade steps based on their computational resources and the minimum key length supported by the victim device.
 Assuming the attacker chooses $n$ downgrade steps, with each step having a consistent interval, the number of computations required by the attacker is $n \times 2^{\frac{128}{n}}$.
When $n$ is 16, the complexity of brute-forcing a 16-byte session key is significantly reduced from $2^{128}$ to $2^{12}$.

The discovery of the UMD attack implies that even if the central device only permits 16-byte session keys, an attacker can still compromise the session key by repeatedly downgrading the key length of peripheral devices. This attack method highlights the inherent risks associated with compatibility with legacy devices, as it enables attackers to easily compromise session keys without actually downgrading them.
